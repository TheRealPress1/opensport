OpenSport athlete photography

The source archive’s src/lib/athleteCredits.json is the canonical asset manifest. It records each photograph's
source title, source URL, photographer, original dimensions, Creative Commons
license, modifications, and height reference. The landing page imports this
manifest for both the composition and its expandable Photo credits section.

The photographs and their cutout adaptations retain the individual licenses
listed in src/lib/athleteCredits.json; these image files are not licensed under the project's
MIT code license. Full license texts are included under licenses/. Keep the
attribution and license with redistributed or modified copies. Background
extraction prompts are retained in the manifest for AI-assisted assets.

The athletes are sporting inspirations. They do not endorse OpenSport, and no
affiliation is claimed. Listed heights guide the composition; the different
poses make this an editorial collage, not a literal height chart.

Font licensing and provenance are kept separately in public/fonts/.
